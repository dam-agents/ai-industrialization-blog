The challenges AI-native ways of working create for design, based on Jamie Jabbour's design pipeline proposal. Examples: building outpaces design validation, AI-generated prototypes are hard to review without a flow, and design tooling lags behind the pace of change. The team doesn't have answers to all of these yet.
