What we do

Agent infrastructure

DAM, our open source platform for running long-lived AI agents — each in an isolated sandbox behind a zero-trust credential gateway, driven from the browser, the CLI, Slack, or on schedules. We're still exploring where the line falls between what the agent handles and what the platform should.

Inference at scale

The serving substrate agentic research runs on, tuned for the throughput and cost of long-running autonomous work — so experiments are bounded by ideas, not by capacity.

Research validation

Interviews, studies, flow boards, coded prototypes — whatever makes an idea concrete enough to put in front of researchers and challenge before it's real. What breaks tells us what to build, change, or stop.
